Saag Gosht

~1 hr · serves 6

0Get everything out first

Food

Spices

Tools

All out. Spinach first.

1Chop and wash

2Blanch the spinach, 2 to 3 minutes

Bring a pot of water to a boil and add 1 tbsp salt. Add the spinach and cook 2 to 3 minutes.

03:00

Drain, rinse with cold water to stop the cooking, then blend into a purée and set it aside.

3Start the curry base

Heat 1 tbsp ghee in the frying pan, then add in order:

4Season it

5Add the spinach, 4 to 5 minutes

Stir in the spinach purée and let it simmer.

05:00

6Brown the lamb

In the second pan, heat the other 1 tbsp ghee. Add the lamb and cook on high until browned on all sides.

7Simmer the lamb, about 15 minutes

Add a little water and bring it to a boil. Cover and simmer until the lamb is almost cooked.

15:00

8Bring it together, 5 to 6 minutes

Stir the spinach gravy into the lamb. If it's too thick, add up to ½ cup hot water. Bring to a boil, then simmer until the curry comes together.

06:00
Dinner's ready. 🎉

9Serve

Serve hot with naan, roti or rice.

Serves 6.
