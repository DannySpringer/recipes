Black Bean Enchiladas

~1 hr · 8–10 enchiladas

0Get everything out first

Use cooked brown rice. If you need to cook it first, start that before anything else.

Filling

Red sauce

Cilantro chutney

To finish

Tools

All out. Three parts: filling, sauce, chutney. Then roll and bake.

1Chop

2Mix the filling

In a bowl, combine the beans, rice, diced onion and jalapeño. Season with 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp chili powder, ½ tsp smoked paprika, salt and pepper. Mix well.

3Make the red sauce

Heat the oil in the saucepan over medium. Cook the onion and garlic until soft and lightly browned. Then:

Simmer about 10 minutes, until the vegetables are soft.

10:00

Blend until smooth. It should be thick but pourable. Taste and adjust the seasoning.

4Blend the chutney

Put everything for the chutney in the blender. Blend until smooth, adding water 1 tbsp at a time (2 to 4 tbsp total). A couple of ice cubes help keep it bright green. Taste and adjust the salt, lime or heat.

5Oven on, tortillas warm

6Roll the enchiladas

7Bake 10 to 15 minutes

Bake until the sauce is bubbling and the cheese has melted. Let it rest a few minutes.

12:00
Dinner's ready. 🎉

8Serve

Drizzle generously with the cilantro chutney. Top with chopped cilantro or a squeeze of lime. A green salad with a light vinaigrette goes well alongside.

Makes 8 to 10 enchiladas.

For vegan, skip the cheese or use a plant-based one. For milder, use less jalapeño and chipotle.

The sauce and chutney keep up to a week in the fridge, so you can make them ahead.
