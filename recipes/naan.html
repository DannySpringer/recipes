Naan

~1¾ hr · makes 8

0Get everything out first

The dough needs about an hour to rise, so start well before dinner.

Food

Tools

All out. Start with the yeast.

1Wake up the yeast, 10 minutes

In the small bowl, stir together the sugar, warm water and yeast. It's ready when it's foamy.

10:00

2Knead the dough, about 10 minutes

Pile the flour on the counter and make a well in the middle. Add the yeast mixture, yogurt, salt and 1 tbsp oil. Knead until the dough is smooth and shiny. A stand mixer works too.

Too sticky? Work in 2 more tbsp flour. Drying out? Add a little water.

10:00

3Let it rise, about 1 hour

Cover with the damp towel and leave it somewhere warm, like next to the stove. It's ready when it has doubled in size. A warm kitchen is faster, a cool one slower.

1:00:00

This timer keeps counting if you close the page, but it can only beep while the page is open. For a long wait, also set an alarm in your phone's Clock app.

4Roll out 8 rounds

Divide the dough into 8 equal pieces. Roll each one into a circle about 8 inches across.

5Cook in a very hot skillet

Heat the skillet over high and wipe on a little oil. Lay in one round. When it puffs up and bubbles and dark spots appear underneath, flip it and cook the other side. Repeat with the rest.

6Butter and serve

Brush each one with melted butter or ghee and sprinkle with cilantro. Serve warm.

Naan's ready. 🎉

Makes 8 naan.
