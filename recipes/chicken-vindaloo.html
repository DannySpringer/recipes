Chicken Vindaloo

~2 hr · serves 4

0Get everything out first

Start the marinade at least an hour before you want to cook. Overnight tastes even better.

For the marinade

For the curry

Tools

All out. Marinade first, then you get at least an hour off.

1Cut and mince

2Make the marinade

Into the large bowl:

Mix until every piece is coated.

3Marinate at least 1 hour

Cover and put it in the fridge. An hour works; overnight is best. While it waits, finely chop the onion.

1:00:00

This timer keeps counting if you close the page, but it can only beep while the page is open. For a long wait, also set an alarm in your phone's Clock app.

4Brown the onion, 5 to 7 minutes

Heat 2 tbsp oil in the skillet over medium. Add the onion and cook until golden brown.

06:00

5Pop the mustard seeds

Add 1 tsp mustard seeds and the dried chilies, if using. Cook about a minute, until the seeds start to pop.

6Brown the chicken, 5 to 7 minutes

Add the marinated chicken. Cook, stirring now and then, until it's browned on all sides.

06:00

7Add the sauce

8Simmer on low, 20 to 25 minutes

Turn the heat to low. It's done when the chicken is cooked through and the sauce has thickened. If it gets too thick, add a splash of water or broth. Taste and add salt if it needs it.

20:00
Dinner's ready. 🎉

9Serve

Sprinkle with chopped cilantro or parsley and serve hot with rice or naan.

Serves 4. Leftovers taste even better the next day. They keep 3 days in the fridge or 2 months in the freezer.
