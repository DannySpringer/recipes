Ground Beef Bulgogi

30 min · serves 2–4

0Get everything out first

A quick, savory Korean-inspired beef bowl for weeknights. Low-sodium soy sauce is best here.

Main

Sauce

For the bowls

Tools

All out. Time to cook.

1Slice the onion

Peel the onion and slice it thin. Set it aside.

2Mix the sauce

In the small bowl, stir the brown sugar, mirin, soy sauce and plum syrup (or the water and vinegar) until the sugar dissolves. Set it near the stove.

3Cook the onion and garlic

Heat a little oil in the skillet over medium-high. Cook the onion for 1 minute, then add the garlic and cook 1 minute more, until fragrant.

4Brown the beef, then add the sauce

Add the beef and break it into small pieces. When it's about a third cooked (still mostly pink), pour in the sauce.

5Simmer until the sauce is gone

Let it simmer, stirring now and then, until the sauce cooks down and the pan is almost dry. The beef should look glossy, not soupy. This takes a little while, but it's worth it.

6Finish with sesame oil

Take the pan off the heat, drizzle with sesame oil and give it a final mix.

7Build the bowls

Spoon the beef over rice. Top with scallions and sesame seeds, and add kimchi or any veggie you like on the side. Enjoy!

Dinner's ready. 🎉

Serves 2 to 4. No plum syrup? Use 1 tbsp water plus 1 tsp apple cider vinegar.
