Rhubarb Upside-Down Cake

~2 hr · 8-inch cake

0Get everything out first

You'll need 12 tbsp butter in all, melted: 4 for the streusel, 2 for the rhubarb, 6 (cooled) for the batter.

Streusel

Rhubarb layer

Cake

Glaze

Tools

All out. Pan first, then three quick mixes.

1Get set up

2Make the streusel

In a medium bowl, stir together the flour, almonds, sugar and salt. Pour in the 4 tbsp melted butter and mix until the crumbs just hold together. Set aside.

3Rhubarb into the pan

In a large bowl, whisk the sugar, cornstarch and lemon zest. Add the rhubarb and stir until every piece is coated. Drizzle with the 2 tbsp melted butter and stir again.

Spread it evenly in the pan, pressing so there are no big gaps. Overlapping is fine.

4Mix the batter

5Batter and streusel on top

Pour the batter over the rhubarb and spread it evenly. Break the streusel into chunks with your fingers and scatter it loosely on top. Don't press it down.

6Bake 45 to 50 minutes

It's done when the top is golden brown and a toothpick in the center comes out clean.

45:00

This timer keeps counting if you close the page, but it can only beep while the page is open. For a long wait, also set an alarm in your phone's Clock app.

7Cool in the pan, 20 minutes

Set the pan on a wire rack.

20:00

8Flip it

Run a thin knife around the edges. Put the platter on top and carefully flip so the rhubarb faces up. Peel off the parchment. Let it cool 10 more minutes.

10:00

9Glaze the rhubarb

Microwave the red currant jelly about 20 seconds, until runny. Gently brush it over the rhubarb.

Cake's ready. 🎉

10Serve

Slice and serve warm or at room temperature, plain or with unsweetened whipped cream.

Makes one 8-inch square cake.
