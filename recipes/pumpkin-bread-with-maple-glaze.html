Pumpkin Bread with Maple Glaze

~2¼ hr · 1 loaf

0Get everything out first

Use pure pumpkin purée, not pumpkin pie filling. Libby's is a safe pick; some other brands are wetter.

Streusel

Bread

Glaze

All out. Streusel first so it can chill.

1Make the streusel

Stir all the streusel ingredients together in the small bowl and put it in the fridge.

2Oven and pan

3Mix the dry ingredients

Whisk the flour, baking soda, cinnamon, pumpkin pie spice and salt in a bowl. Set it aside.

4Mix the wet ingredients

5Bring it together

Mix in, in this order:

6Fill the pan and add the streusel

Pour the batter into the pan and spread it out evenly. Break the chilled streusel into even crumbs with a fork and cover the whole top with it.

7Bake 1 hour 20 minutes

It's done when a toothpick in the center comes out clean. If not, give it 5 more minutes at a time. A very full pan can stay wet in the middle, so trust the toothpick, not the clock.

1:20:00

This timer keeps counting if you close the page, but it can only beep while the page is open. For a long wait, also set an alarm in your phone's Clock app.

8Make the glaze while it bakes

Whisk the powdered sugar and maple syrup together until smooth.

9Glaze it hot

As soon as the bread comes out of the oven, pour the glaze over the hot loaf.

10Cool 20 minutes, then slice

Let it cool about 20 minutes. Lift it out by the parchment and slice.

20:00
Bread's ready. 🎉

Makes 1 loaf. Serve warm. Keep leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge.
