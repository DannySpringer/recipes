Check each one off as it goes in. Keep the greens aside for now.

3Greens on top

Pile the greens on top and push them down into the water as much as you can. They won't all go under, and that's fine. The tops will steam.

4Cook on HIGH, about 4 hours

Lid on, set to high. It's done when the beans are soft all the way through. Older beans or a gentler slow cooker can take longer, so taste a few before moving on.
