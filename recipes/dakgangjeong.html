Dakgangjeong

1½ hr · serves 4–6

0Get everything out first

These are deep-fried twice. A heavy pot and a thermometer make it much easier.

For the sauce

For the wings

No gochujang? Use the same amount of sriracha, and only 2 tbsp water in the sauce.

Tools

Everything's out. Sauce first, then the oil.

1Prep the wings and aromatics

2Make the sauce

In a large bowl, microwave the sesame oil, garlic and ginger for 40 to 60 seconds, until bubbly and fragrant but not browned. Then whisk in:

Whisk until smooth and set it aside.

3Heat the oil to 350°F

Heat the oil in the Dutch oven over medium-high to 350°F. While it heats, whisk the batter in the second bowl:

Whisk until smooth, and set the wire rack in the baking sheet.

4First fry, batch 1, about 7 minutes

Stir half the wings into the batter. With tongs, lift them out one at a time, let the extra batter drip off, and lower them into the oil. Turn the heat to high. Stir now and then so they don't stick. The oil temperature will drop a lot; that's normal. They're done when light golden and starting to crisp. Move them to the rack.

07:00

5First fry, batch 2, about 7 minutes

Bring the oil back to 350°F and do the same with the rest of the wings.

07:00

Then turn the heat to medium and let this batch rest 5 minutes.

05:00

6Second fry at 375°F, about 7 minutes

Heat the oil to 375°F. Carefully put all the wings back in and cook, stirring now and then, until deep golden brown and very crispy. Back onto the rack for 2 minutes.

07:00

7Toss in the sauce

Toss the wings in the sauce bowl until coated. Put them back on the rack for 2 minutes so the coating sets, then pile them onto a platter.

02:00
Wings are ready. 🎉

Serves 4 to 6 as a main dish. Good with steamed white rice and a slaw.
