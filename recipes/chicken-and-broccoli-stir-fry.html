Chicken & Broccoli Stir Fry

30 min · serves 6

1Marinate the chicken

Cut the chicken into 1½" pieces. Larger pieces stay juicier. Mix the marinade in the bowl or bag, add the chicken and toss to coat. Leave it at room temperature for 15 to 30 minutes, no longer.

15:00

This timer keeps counting if you close the page, but it can only beep while the page is open.

2Prep the vegetables

While the chicken marinates, cut the broccoli into bite-sized florets and mince the garlic and ginger.

3Whisk the sauce

Whisk all the sauce ingredients together in the small bowl and set it near the stove.

4Brown the chicken 2–3 minutes

Heat 1 tbsp oil in the skillet over medium-high until very hot. It should sizzle but not smoke heavily. Add the chicken and stir fry until golden. It won't be cooked through yet. Move it to the plate.

03:00

5Cook the broccoli

Heat the other 1 tbsp oil in the same skillet until it sizzles. Stir fry the broccoli for 30 seconds, then add the rest and cook 1 minute more.

6Add the chicken and sauce

Put the chicken back in the pan. Whisk the sauce again and pour it in. Stir constantly for 1 to 2 minutes, until the sauce is thick, the chicken is cooked through and the broccoli is as tender as you like.

02:00

7Serve

Spoon it over rice. Top with green onions and sesame seeds if you like. If it tastes like something's missing, add more chili sauce.

Dinner's ready. 🎉

Tips

  • Low-sodium broth and soy sauce. Use both, or the dish gets too salty.
  • Oyster sauce. Use a good brand like Lee Kum Kee or Kikkoman. The quality makes a big difference.
  • Rice wine. Don't swap in rice vinegar. Vinegar makes it sour, while rice wine adds sweetness and depth. Aji-Mirin works well.
  • No fresh garlic or ginger. Whisk 1 tsp each of garlic powder and ground ginger into the sauce instead.
  • Prep first. Have everything ready before you start, because the cooking only takes a few minutes.
  • Chicken size. Cut the pieces on the larger side (1 to 2") so they don't dry out.
  • Pan heat. The chicken should sizzle, but the oil shouldn't smoke heavily. If it does, take the pan off the heat for a moment to cool.
  • Oil. Use a neutral oil, not olive oil.
  • Don't overcook the chicken. Just brown it. It finishes cooking in the sauce.
  • Don't overcook the broccoli. It keeps cooking in the sauce, so take it off a little early rather than late.
  • Heat level. If it tastes like something's missing, add more chili sauce.
  • Other veggies. Swap in other vegetables for some or all of the broccoli.

Make ahead

Up to 48 hours ahead, store each of these in its own container in the fridge:

  • The whisked sauce (whisk it again before using)
  • The marinade ingredients in a zip-top bag
  • The chopped chicken
  • The chopped broccoli, garlic and ginger

When you're ready, start cooking from step 1.

Freezing

Let it cool completely. Store it in an airtight container or freezer bag with the air pressed out for 2 to 3 months. Thaw in the fridge, then reheat.

Serves 6. Prep 20 minutes, cook 10. Serve over rice or cauliflower rice.
