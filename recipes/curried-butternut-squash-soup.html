Curried Butternut Squash Soup

30 min · serves 4

0Get everything out first

One small butternut squash gives about 6 cups once it's peeled and chopped. Chop everything before you start; the cooking goes fast.

To serve (optional)

Tools

All out. Time to cook.

1Heat the pot

Put the large pot over medium heat and let it get hot.

2Sauté shallots and garlic 2 minutes

Add, then stir often for 2 minutes:

02:00

3Add the squash and spices

Add these and stir so the squash is coated. Cover and cook 4 minutes, stirring now and then.

04:00

4Pour in the liquids

5Simmer 15 minutes

Bring it to a low boil over medium heat. Then turn the heat to low, cover, and simmer about 15 minutes. It's ready when a fork slides easily into the squash.

15:00

This timer keeps counting if you close the page, but it can only beep while the page is open.

6Blend until smooth

Blend right in the pot with an immersion blender until creamy. If you use a regular blender, blend on high in batches and pour it back into the pot. Hot soup expands, so don't fill the blender more than halfway and hold the lid down with a towel.

7Taste and adjust

Taste it. Add more curry powder, salt or maple syrup if it needs it. Let it cook a few more minutes over medium heat.

8Serve

Ladle into bowls. Serve it plain, or top with pumpkin seeds, a little chili garlic paste, or a swirl of full-fat coconut milk.

Soup's on. 🎉

Serves 4. Best fresh. Keeps 3–4 days covered in the fridge, or up to 1 month in the freezer.

About 287 calories a serving, with 5 g protein and 12 g fiber.
