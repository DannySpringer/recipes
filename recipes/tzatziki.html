Tzatziki

5 min · one bowl

0Get everything out first

So you don't have to go looking mid-recipe.

Everything's out? Good. That was the hardest part.

1Grate the cucumber

Use the big holes on the grater. Stop when you have about ½ cup.

2Squeeze it

Put the cucumber in the towel and squeeze it over the sink. Don't skip this, or the dip turns watery.

3Dump it all in the bowl

Check each one off as it goes in.

4Stir

Stir until it's all mixed.

It's ready to eat now. 🎉

5Chill it (optional)

Put it in the fridge if you want it cold. Set a timer so you don't forget it's there.

30:00

This timer only runs while this page is open. For a long chill, your phone's Clock app is safer.

Eat it with pita, raw veggies, or on sandwiches and falafel.
